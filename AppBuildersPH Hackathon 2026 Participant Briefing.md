THE THEME 

# **<mark>Local AI</mark>** 

Useful AI experiences where meaningful AI computation happens on the user's device, rather than depending entirely on cloud inference. This is different from building an AI product for a local audience. 

05 / 29 

AppBuildersPH Hackathon 2026 · Local AI 

IN SCOPE 

###### **<mark>What counts as Local AI</mark>** 

Local LLMs Local vision models Local speech recognition Local embeddings and RAG Local AI agents Local image generation Edge AI Offline AI Privacy-preserving AI Hybrid local + cloud systems AI on PCs, laptops, phones, or edge hardware 

Cloud services may be used, but meaningful AI functionality must run locally.

THE CHALLENGE 

**Build an AI product that remains genuinely useful when the cloud disappears.** 

THE CHALLENGE 

###### **<mark>What we're asking for</mark>** 

Create a working product that uses AI running locally on a user's device to solve a real problem. 

Show why running AI locally creates an experience that would be difficult, expensive, slow, private, or impossible with a cloud-only approach.
